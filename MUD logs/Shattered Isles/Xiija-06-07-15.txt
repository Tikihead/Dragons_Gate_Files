[Crowns Pass.]

Rocks of varying shapes and sizes lie scattered randomly upon the road making travel with wagon or cart difficult.  The large cliff faces of the mountain stand proudly above the road as if boasting their existence to travelers.  Large footprints, perhaps left by ogre or troll, can be seen imprinted in the dirt.
